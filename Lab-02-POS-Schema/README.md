## 📖 Overview
This repository contains the schema design, sample data, and analytical queries for a robust **Point of Sale (POS) System**. The database is engineered to handle complex business operations, including Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), inventory tracking, order management, and dynamic discount applications[cite: 4].

## 🗄️ Schema Architecture
The database is highly normalized and divided into three core operational sectors:

| Sector | Tables | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Security & Auth** | `roles`, `permissions`, `role_permissions`, `users` | Manages Admins, Salesmen, and Customers with granular system permissions[cite: 4]. |
| **Inventory** | `categories`, `products`, `inventory_logs` | Tracks product details, categorizations, and every stock adjustment made by users[cite: 4]. |
| **Sales & Billing** | `orders`, `order_items`, `discount_rules` | Records transaction history, line-item details, and time-sensitive promotional discounts[cite: 4]. |

## 📊 Analytics & Reporting
The script includes 10 complex reporting queries utilizing `JOIN`, `GROUP BY`, and aggregate functions to extract business intelligence, such as[cite: 4]:
* **Revenue Tracking:** Total spent per customer and total revenue generated by category[cite: 4].
* **Inventory Insights:** Best-selling products by quantity and full audit trails of stock changes[cite: 4].
* **Sales Operations:** Evaluating the financial impact of discounted vs. non-discounted orders[cite: 4].

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Open your local SQL environment (e.g., XAMPP phpMyAdmin, MySQL Workbench).
2. Execute the `Lab 02_Mahdi Ali_16.sql` script.
3. The script will automatically build the `Point_of_Sale` database, populate all 10 tables, and output the business intelligence reports[cite: 4].
